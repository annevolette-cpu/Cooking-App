## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, mindful approach to culinary creation. Built for home cooks, bakers, and food lovers seeking inspiration without the high-stress, noisy environment of traditional recipe blogs and fast-paced social platforms.

### Personality & Tone
- **Atmospheric & Grounded:** Clean porcelain backgrounds, muted herbal accents, and quiet twilight tones replace aggressive kitchen reds and yellows.
- **Gender-Neutral Culinary Refinement:** Balancing cool slate and dried lavender with vegetal sage mist and warm oatmeal avoids floral sweetness, delivering an artisanal, contemporary atmosphere.
- **Mindful & Focused:** Generous negative space and restrained contrast allow food photography, step-by-step instructions, and community dialogue to breathe without visual clutter.

### Aesthetic Direction
A minimalist, tactile modern aesthetic characterized by subtle surface shifts, airy container hierarchies, delicate tone-on-tone borders, and friendly rounded contours.

## Colors

The palette relies on desaturated, herbal, and mineral pastels layered over warm ceramic neutrals to maintain a tranquil, non-gendered look.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#7D7098` — Muted Dried Lavender):** Key focal points, primary action buttons, active navigation markers, and headline branding accents.
- **Secondary (`#5B74A8` — Pastel Cornflower Slate):** Interactive metadata, timers, unit toggles, community mentions, and secondary interaction indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#8AA39B` — Sage Mist):** Ingredient badges, dietary tags (vegan, gluten-free), success confirmations, and fresh freshness indicators.
- **Neutral Surface Canvas (`#F7F7FA` — Soft Porcelain):** Root background offering a clean, calm reading plane.
- **Neutral Container Base (`#F0F1F6` — Warm Oatmeal Tint):** Grouping containers, recipe cards, input fields, and quiet divider strokes.
- **Text & Contrast:** 
  - Primary text: Deep Charcoal Slate (`#262730`) ensures effortless readability in ambient kitchen lighting without harsh true black.
  - Secondary text: Muted Heather (`#636574`) for measurements, timestamps, and secondary captions.

## Typography

The type scale pairs the structured, editorial flair of **Epilogue** for display titles with the open, warm clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for running body copy and procedural cooking steps.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Headlines:** Use `Epilogue` with tight tracking for recipe titles, collection labels, and editorial community features. Its contemporary proportions give warmth without ornamental fuss.
- **Body & Preparation Steps:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides open apertures and generous x-height, maintaining high legibility on tablets propped against kitchen counters.
- **Labels & Timers:** Render measurements, preparation timings, and nutritional callouts in `label-md` or `label-lg` with a medium or semi-bold weight to keep key data legible at a glance.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid column grid provides responsive flexibility across devices, prioritizing generous whitespace to avoid cognitive overload during cooking.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Mobile (Up to 599px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer margin and `1.25rem` gutters. Step cards, timers, and ingredient check-lists stretch full width.
- **Tablet (600px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margin. Allows side-by-side display of ingredients and active cooking instructions.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px` centered within `3.5rem` margins. Three-column card grids for community feeds; split-pane viewing for full-page recipes.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids dark, heavy dropshadows in favor of tonal surface tiers and diffused, tinted ambient light.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#F7F7FA` base surface.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Groups):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers nested over porcelain canvas, outlined with a hairline border (`1px solid #E8E9F0`).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Floats, Interactive Cards):** Tinted ambient glow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(125, 112, 152, 0.08)`. The primary lavender tint keeps the elevation soft and cohesive.
- **Level 3 (Modal Overlays, Persistent Kitchen Timers):** `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(91, 116, 168, 0.14)` combined with a frosted backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a 40% translucent porcelain scrim).

## Shapes

The design leverages approachable, organic geometry that feels soft in the hand without appearing childish or excessively novelty-driven.

- **Base Components (Input fields, Cards, Image frames):** Styled with `rounded` (0.5rem) to `rounded-lg` (1rem).
- **Pill Form Factors:** Badges, chips, floating timers, and call-to-action buttons use full pill rounding (`9999px`) to create an inviting, tactile rhythm throughout the UI.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#7D7098` pill with pure white text. Hover transitions to `#6F6289`. Active press shifts scale slightly (98%). Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`.
- **Secondary:** Filled `#EAE6F2` (soft lavender wash) with `#4D4263` text, pill shape, zero border.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `#5B74A8` label and rounded container corners (`0.5rem`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Small (`0.25rem 0.75rem`) pill containers. 
  - *Dietary / Botanical (Sage):* Background `#EBF1EF`, text `#3D544C`.
  - *Timers / Technique (Slate):* Background `#EAF0F9`, text `#364A70`.
  - *Community / Notes (Lavender):* Background `#F2EFF8`, text `#574B70`.

### Recipe Lists & Checklists
- **Ingredient Checklist:** Minimal row items separated by subtle hairline rules (`#F0F1F6`). Checking an item triggers an animated strike-through and changes the text color to a subdued `#9E9FA9`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded squares (radii of `0.375rem`) and circular radios with soft sage (`#8AA39B`) active states and clean white glyphs.

### Inputs & Fields
- **Search & Text Entry:** Porcelain-filled containers (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` border in `#E2E3EC`. Focus states transition to a `1.5px` border in `#5B74A8` paired with a soft `3px` focus ring tinted with 15% opacity slate blue.

### Recipe Cards
- Pure white container with `1rem` corner rounding, subtle `1px` boundary border (`#EAEBF2`), and an edge-to-edge top aspect ratio (4:3) image. Metadata pills float within an overlay or sit cleanly beneath the recipe title in a horizontal flow.

### Cook Mode Floating Bar (App-Specific Component)
- A floating pill-shaped action deck anchored to the lower viewport. Uses blurred porcelain glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), holding persistent hands-free timers, voice control toggles, and step progress indicators.
